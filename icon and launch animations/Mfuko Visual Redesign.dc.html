Coins Stacking Sequence

~2.0s total. Four coins rise into frame one by one from bottom — the chama pooling in real-time — then "Mfuko" settles into view. Native Compose, no Lottie.

Native Compose Implementation
Coin shapes via Canvas + drawOval (same primitive as HealthGauge, §6.7). Each coin driven by an Animatable<Float> with spring(DampingRatioMediumBouncy) on the vertical offset, launched with delay(stagger × coinIndex) inside LaunchedEffect. Splash window handled by androidx.core.splashscreen. Zero new runtime dependencies.

Icon & Mark Rules
→4 stacked coins: bottom 3 = member contributions (green ramp), top coin = gold harvest
→Wordmark: Bricolage Grotesque SemiBold 600, always "Mfuko" — never all-caps or italic
→Symbol: splash screen and icon only — not in the in-app TopAppBar
→Clear space: minimum = cap-height of "M" on all sides
→Never: gradient fill, partial stack (must show all 4 coins), single-color gold-only

Brand Principles
1.Money is legible first — largest weight in context, tabular figures, nothing competing.
2.Warmth over coldness — deep green, raw gold, sun-baked terracotta. Not clinical blue.
3.Trust through restraint — one accent per screen, generous whitespace, motion felt not noticed.
4.Communal not corporate — the chama is people building together, not an institution.
5.Status at a glance — color + shape always, never just bold text, never just a word.
